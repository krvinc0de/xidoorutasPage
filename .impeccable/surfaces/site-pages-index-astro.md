# Inicio · Xidoo Rutas

Scope: the home page (`site/pages/index.astro`). Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: salmantinos who ride public transport, usually on the phone while waiting at a stop. They should understand that Xidoo Rutas is coming back, see what it will do (rutas/horarios/paradas/costos, Ruta Cultural, Publicidad Local), and **follow the project on social media** while the app is not available yet. Secondary: link out to the other site pages.

Proof on hand: CONIES 2021 second place nationally; the 2020–2022 history; the five developers; the 2021 screenshots; the video (YouTube yP6a73tT6c8). No downloads, store links, merchant registration or coverage claims.

Constraints: Astro (srcDir `site/`), legacy pages copied as-is into the build. The Xidoo yellow (#FFBD59) and the bus logo are fixed.

Open: launch date, app stores, when the legacy pages get migrated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page reads like the windshield of a Salamanca bus. Visitors know at a glance whether this route is theirs from the fluorescent cardboard signs stacked behind the glass. It refuses the category default: a headline next to a phone mockup with colored lines and store badges.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is dark, greenish laminated windshield glass with a tinted sun strip across the top. That strip holds the vinyl navigation. Fluorescent cardboard signs in Xidoo yellow, day-glo orange, green and pink carry every headline in a condensed sign-painter cap face. Notes are in marker and things are fastened with tape. Printed notices and the operator's ID card get taped to the glass. Body text is Atkinson Hyperlegible.

STORY: I see the signs and get it right away: rutas, cultura, negocios, all of Salamanca. I read that it's coming back, and I follow it on Instagram, Facebook or X.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The sun strip runs across the top with the logo and links. A stack of slightly crooked signs sits below it. The main sign is huge and yellow with the headline. Under it are three smaller destination signs, one per service. A taped white sign carries the three social buttons, which are the primary action, and a marker note: "Próximamente".

FORM: Letreros de parabrisas, #1 on the grounded list (pick). seed:1741b0b1f6c686a0. Signature interaction: tapping a destination sign swaps the main sign the way a driver swaps the cardboard. It drops and settles with a slight swing.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
